The low air pressure warning must come on before pressure in the service air tank drops below about:
40 psiYour answer
60 psiCorrect answer

The answer is B · 60 psi
Federal rules require a warning you can see or hear before tank pressure falls below 60 psi (or half the compressor governor cut-out pressure, if that is lower). If it comes on, stop safely as soon as you can.
